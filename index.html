I'm Yash Sharma, the Founder of Hackiware and a final-year BCA (Cybersecurity Specialization) student at Poornima University. My passion for cybersecurity goes beyond academics — it's about creating real impact. I focus on Ethical Hacking, Digital Forensics, VAPT (Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing), and Security Awareness, turning my curiosity into action.

Over the years, I've had the privilege of delivering 30+ seminars across schools and educational institutions, where I've trained more than 10,000 students in cybersecurity concepts and safe digital practices. Through initiatives like Kavach Suraksha ke 7 Din and Source Code Seminar, I've worked to build a more cyber-aware community and inspire the next generation of ethical hackers.

I'm naturally curious and love staying ahead of the curve — exploring emerging technologies, developing innovative tools, and working on projects that strengthen security for individuals and organizations alike.
